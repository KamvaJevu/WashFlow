# WashFlow

### Smart Cleaning Business Management System

WashFlow is a multi-tenant business management platform
designed to help independent cleaning businesses manage
their daily operations through one centralised system.

## Project Overview

The platform is designed for businesses such as mobile car
washes and dustbin cleaning services.

It aims to simplify business operations by providing tools
for customer management, service management, bookings,
job scheduling, payments and reporting.

## Core Features

- Multi-business registration and management
- User authentication and authorisation
- Customer management
- Vehicle management
- Service and pricing management
- Booking and scheduling
- Job management and staff assignment
- Payment tracking
- Expense management
- Business reporting and analytics

## Architecture

WashFlow will follow a multi-tenant architecture.

Multiple independent cleaning businesses will operate
on the same platform while maintaining separation of
their business data.
